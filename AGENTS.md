# KittyCraft

Pure-Rust ArtCraft workstation for cat & kitten advice, breed matching, medical/toxicology guidance, legal/vaccination rules, diary, sitter calendar, and `Ctrl+J` skill-preprompt AI chat.

- Build & verify: `cargo fmt --all --check`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace`
- Launch: `.\start.ps1` or `.\start.ps1 -Smoke`
